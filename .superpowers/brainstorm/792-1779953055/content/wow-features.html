Which "wow" features excite you?

Click any/all that sound great. Selected ones turn green. You can also tell me in chat which ones are must-haves vs. nice-to-haves.

🔔

Push notifications

Phone buzzes when your team's match starts, when a goal is scored, or at full-time. Huge for fans watching from home.

🎯

Bracket predictions

Users predict the bracket before kickoff. Leaderboard for who's the best predictor. Drives daily opens during the event.

🏆

MVP & Goal-of-the-Tournament voting

Vote per-match for MVP. End of tournament: community-voted MVP, Goal of the Tournament, Save of the Tournament.

📲

One-tap share graphic

After every match, share a branded score card to WhatsApp / Instagram. Free marketing every match.

📺

Live match streaming

Embed YouTube / Twitch stream on the match-detail page. Watch live + see scores in one place.

📸

Photo & video gallery

Community uploads photos / clips per match. Players love seeing themselves; fans relive the moment.

💬

Live match chat

Per-match chat feed during the game. Friends comment as it happens. Big time-in-app boost.

📋

Per-field QR scoring

Print a QR per match. Referee scans, opens a focused scoring screen for that match. No admin login. You loved this idea.

✅

Player check-in

Players check in when they arrive. Coaches see who's there. Great for tournament logistics.

📣

Tournament announcements

Dedicated feed for venue updates ("Field 2 delayed", "Awards at 7pm"). Push-notified to attendees.
