Three approaches to fit September 4–7

Same total vision — different phasing strategies. Click the one you want as our anchor.

A

"Tournament-Ready MVP"   ⭐ Recommended

Goal: September 4–7 is magical. Everything else ships in the months after.

Phase 1 — By Sept 4 (must-haves)
  • Critical bug fixes + perf (no crashes, fast loading)
  • Admin app tournament CRUD (you can actually run the event)
  • Live bracket + live scores that update in real time
  • Push notifications (team plays / scores / final)
  • QR per-field scoring for referees
  • One-tap share match card to WhatsApp
  • Tournament announcements feed
Phase 2 — Sept–Dec 2026
  • Photo & video gallery
  • MVP / Goal-of-the-Tournament voting
  • Player check-in
  • Bracket predictions (ready for next tournament)
Phase 3 — Jan–Apr 2027
  • Historical archive + cross-tournament career stats
  • Live match chat (needs moderation tooling)
  • Live stream embedding

Risk: Low. The September event has the things that matter most for tournament-day excitement. Everything else slots in cleanly afterward.

B

"Foundation & Showcase"

Goal: Rock-solid base, then 2–3 features that hit hard. Quality over quantity.

Phase 1 — By Sept 4
  • Bug fixes + perf + tech-debt cleanup
  • Polished admin app for tournament CRUD
  • Live bracket + scores (no fancy extras)
  • Push notifications
  • One showcase wow feature (your pick — QR scoring, share card, or predictions)

Risk: Low. Safest, but the tournament weekend feels less ambitious. More features ship in Phase 2 onwards.

C

"Maximum scope, ship what's ready"

Goal: Build everything in parallel. Whatever's done by Sept 4 ships; rest follows.

  • No prioritization — we work breadth-first across all features
  • Sept 4 ships with whatever's ready, even if half-finished
  • Possible that critical features are unpolished or buggy at tournament time

Risk: High. This is how products ship broken. The tournament weekend is real and visible; a buggy app reflects on you. I do not recommend this.

My recommendation: A. The full vision still gets built — just in three waves. Sept 4 hits with everything that matters for tournament excitement. Phases 2 & 3 land the rest while you have breathing room.
