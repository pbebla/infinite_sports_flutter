Infinite Sports App – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Infinite Sports Association ("Infinite Sports", "we", "us") is a nonprofit based in San Jose, California. We run the Infinite Sports mobile app (the "App") for our sports leagues, tournaments, community events and the Infinite Fantasy game. This policy explains what information the App collects, how we use it, what other people can see, and the choices you have.

Questions or requests: contact@infinitesports.org.

1. Information you give us

  • Account details. First and last name, email address, phone number, and a password if you sign up with email. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • Sign in with Google or Apple. If you choose one of these, Google or Apple sends us your name and email address (Apple may send a private relay address instead of your real one). We never receive your Google or Apple password.
  • About you. Date of birth, gender, city, ZIP code, and how you heard about us. We ask once, when you create your account.
  • Profile. An optional profile photo and optional player details such as positions, height and age.
  • League and tournament registration. Your answers on the registration form (these vary by league and can include your position, shirt size, age and an emergency contact), your acknowledgement of the waiver and rules, any promo code, and whether you have paid.
  • Payments. Card payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe collects your card details directly; we receive only a confirmation, the amount and a payment reference, never your full card number. If you pay with Venmo or Zelle, that happens outside the App; we record that you told us you paid and when we confirmed it.
  • Infinite Fantasy. Your team name, the players you pick, transfers, chips, points, rankings, and the mini-leagues you create or join.
  • Events. Events you mark yourself as attending.
  • Support. The messages and screenshots you send through Help & Feedback. The App automatically adds your name, email, app version, phone platform and model, and the screen you were on, so we can help.
  • Polls and predictions. Votes you cast in game polls and the predictions you make on matches. They are recorded against your account so you can only vote once; nobody else sees how you voted.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token. A device token so we can deliver the notifications you turn on.
  • Device and app details. Phone type, operating system version and app version, plus the IP address your device uses to reach our servers (handled by Google Firebase as part of running the service).
  • Preferences. The sports and events you follow and your notification choices.
  • Location. If you allow it, the App uses your device's location to center the Around You map on nearby businesses and events. Location is used on your phone only; we don't store it on our servers.
  • Camera and photos. If you allow it, to set a profile photo or attach a screenshot to a support request.

We don't use advertising trackers or third-party analytics tools in the App.

3. Information recorded about your play

League staff record game results and player statistics (such as goals, assists, points, cards and awards) and your team and season history. These are the league's official records.

4. How we use information

  • To run leagues, tournaments and events: registration, rosters, schedules, scores, standings, statistics and awards.
  • To take and confirm payments and send receipts.
  • To run Infinite Fantasy: scoring, rankings, price changes, badges and fantasy news.
  • To send the notifications and messages you choose, and service messages about your account, registrations and payments.
  • To answer support requests and improve the App.
  • To combine duplicate accounts that belong to the same person (section 8).
  • To keep the App safe: preventing fraud, abuse and cheating, and enforcing our Terms of Use.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

5. What other people can see

Infinite Sports is a community sports league, so much of what happens on the field is public in the App, and some of it can be seen without signing in:

  • Your name, profile photo, and any player details you add (positions, height, age).
  • The teams and seasons you have played in, tournaments, game statistics and awards.
  • League rosters, schedules, results and standings.
  • Your fantasy team name, points, rankings, badges, the mini-leagues you are in, and your picks once each week's deadline has passed.
  • Fantasy news posts, which may feature players by name with their statistics.
  • Match cards that you or other people choose to share outside the App, showing team names, scores and featured players.

Not public: your email, phone number, date of birth, city, ZIP code, gender, payment details, support requests, and private registration answers such as emergency contacts. League staff can see registration details in order to run the league.

6. Who we share information with

We share information only with the services that help us run the App, with league staff, or when the law requires it. We don't sell personal information and we don't share it with anyone for their own advertising.

  • Google Firebase – sign-in, database, file storage, push notifications, server functions and web hosting. Data is stored on Google's servers in the United States.
  • Google Maps – the Around You map.
  • Google and Apple – sign-in, if you choose them.
  • Stripe – card payments.
  • Venmo and Zelle – if you choose them, you pay in their apps under their own terms.
  • Anthropic – we use Anthropic's AI service to draft fantasy news articles from public game and fantasy statistics (a player's name, team, position, points, price and photo link). We never send contact details, birthdates, payment information or anything else private, and drafts are reviewed before they are published. Under Anthropic's commercial terms this data is not used to train its models.
  • Our email provider – for receipts, support replies and account notices sent from infinitesports.org addresses.
  • League staff and referees – use an internal management app to see the registrations, rosters, payments and support requests needed to run the league.
  • Legal – when required by law or a court order, or to protect the safety of players and the community.

7. Notifications and messages

  • Push notifications. You decide whether to allow them when the App asks. In Settings you can turn off all notifications or choose categories; announcements and promotions can be turned off there too.
  • Email. We send account, registration, payment and support emails. We don't send marketing email without asking you first.
  • Text messages. If you give us your phone number and agree to receive texts, we may text you about schedules, registration, reminders and league updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. We don't share phone numbers with third parties for their marketing.

8. Duplicate accounts

If you end up with two accounts (for example, you signed up again with another email address), we may combine them into one so your history stays together. We tell you by email, by notification and in the App which account to keep using, and we turn off sign-in on the old one.

9. Children

You must be at least 13 to create an account. Players under 18 may be registered for a league only by a parent or legal guardian, who accepts the league waiver and our Terms of Use on the player's behalf and agrees that the player's name, team and statistics appear in the App like any other player's. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, or you want your child's information removed, email contact@infinitesports.org and we will take care of it.

10. Your choices

  • Update your details in Profile and Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete Account, or email contact@infinitesports.org from your account's email address. We delete your account, sign-in, contact details, profile, date of birth and other personal details within 30 days. Payment records are kept as long as our accounting rules require. League records (rosters, results and statistics) are the league's history and remain; on request we remove your photo and personal details from them and can replace your name with a generic entry. More at infinite-sports-app.web.app/delete-account.
  • Permissions. Location, camera and photo access can be turned off in your phone's settings. The App keeps working, except for map centering and photo features.
  • Notifications in Settings → Notifications.
  • Access or correct. Email us and we will send you the information we hold about you or correct it.

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, to have it deleted, and not to be treated differently for asking. We don't sell personal information or share it for advertising. Use the contact email above for any request.

11. How long we keep information

Account information while your account exists and for up to 30 days after you delete it. Support requests for up to two years, or until you delete your account. Payment records as required for our accounting, typically seven years. League records indefinitely, as described above. Backups of our database may hold deleted data for a short time afterwards.

12. Security

Your information is stored with Google Firebase, encrypted in transit and at rest, and protected by access rules so that private details are readable only by you and league staff. Staff access is limited to what is needed to run the league. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will notify you as the law requires.

13. Changes to this policy

We post updates here with a new date and tell you in the App about important changes. Using the App after a change means you accept it.
