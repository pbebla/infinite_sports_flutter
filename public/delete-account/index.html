Delete your Infinite Sports account

Infinite Sports Association · the Infinite Sports app

In the app

  1. Open the menu and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm and tap Delete my account.

The app asks you to sign in again first (your password, Google or Apple). That is a security check, not an error.

By email

Send a request from the email address on your account to contact@infinitesports.org with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email, Google or Apple) and password.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, city, ZIP code and profile photo.
  • Your player profile details and public profile.
  • Your fantasy teams, picks and mini-league memberships.
  • Your notification tokens and preferences.
  • Your support requests.

What is kept

  • Payment records, for as long as our accounting rules require (typically seven years).
  • League records (rosters, results, standings and statistics), which are the league's history. Your photo and sign-in link are removed from them automatically; on request we can replace your name with a generic entry.
  • Poll votes and match predictions, which stay as anonymous entries.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
